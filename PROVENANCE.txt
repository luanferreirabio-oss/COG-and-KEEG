COG REFERENCE TABLES — PROVENANCE
=================================

Files
-----
cog-24.def.tab   5,050 COGs. Columns: 1 COG ID | 2 functional category letter(s), in
                 order of importance | 3 COG name | 4 gene name | 5 metabolic pathway |
                 6 PubMed ID | 7 PDB ID

cog-24.fun.tab   25 functional categories. Columns: 1 category letter | 2 functional
                 group (1-4) | 3 RGB hex colour | 4 category description

Source
------
COG-2024 release of the Clusters of Orthologous Genes database, NCBI.

    https://ftp.ncbi.nih.gov/pub/COG/COG2024/data/cog-24.def.tab
    https://ftp.ncbi.nih.gov/pub/COG/COG2024/data/cog-24.fun.tab
    https://ftp.ncbi.nih.gov/pub/COG/COG2024/data/Readme.COG2024.txt

Access date: 25 August 2026.

The COG database receives continuous updates within a release. Re-downloading these
tables at a later date may yield marginally different annotations. The access date is
therefore part of the provenance of the result and is stated in the Methods.
